**ROLE SUMMARY**
The Management and Program Analyst serves in Program Analysis and Evaluation (PAE), Office of Resource Management, Office of Construction and Facilities Management, Office of Acquisition Logistics and Construction. The Service promotes optimal construction program management by developing business solutions that maximize support to VHA, NCA and VBA projects by aligning solution with business needs. The position provides analysis and reports to CFM, NCA and VHA on a variety of topics.

**KEY SKILLS REQUIRED**
- Analyzing and evaluating data to identify trends and patterns
- Developing business solutions that align with business needs
- Providing analysis and reports to stakeholders
- Maximizing support to VHA, NCA and VBA projects
- Aligning solutions with business needs
- Promoting optimal construction program management
- Developing and implementing business solutions

**QUALIFICATIONS AND ELIGIBILITY**
- Citizenship: Must be a U.S. citizen to be considered for this position
- Education: A bachelor's degree from an accredited college or university with a major in business administration, public administration, management, engineering, architecture, or a related field
- Experience: A minimum of 4 years of experience in a field related to the position, such as business administration, public administration, management, engineering, architecture, or a related field
- Specialized Experience: A minimum of 1 year of specialized experience in a field related to the position, such as business administration, public administration, management, engineering, architecture, or a related field
- Certification: Certification in a field related to the position, such as business administration, public administration, management, engineering, architecture, or a related field
- Other Requirements: Must be able to obtain and maintain a security clearance

**RESPONSIBILITIES**
- Provides analysis and reports to CFM, NCA and VHA on a variety of topics
- Develops business solutions that maximize support to VHA, NCA and VBA projects
- Aligns solutions with business needs to promote optimal construction program management
- Analyzes and evaluates data to identify trends and patterns
- Develops and implements business solutions to support VHA, NCA and VBA projects
- Provides recommendations to stakeholders on business solutions and program management
- Collaborates with stakeholders to develop and implement business solutions
- Analyzes and evaluates the effectiveness of business solutions and program management
- Develops and implements plans to improve business solutions and program management.
