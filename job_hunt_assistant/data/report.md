**ROLE SUMMARY**

The Management and Program Analyst serves in Program Analysis and Evaluation (PAE), Office of Resource Management, Office of Construction and Facilities Management, Office of Acquisition Logistics and Construction. The Service promotes optimal construction program management by developing business solutions that maximize support to VHA, NCA and VBA projects by aligning solution with business needs. The position provides analysis and reports to CFM, NCA and VHA on a variety of topics.

**KEY SKILLS REQUIRED**

- Develop business solutions that maximize support to VHA, NCA and VBA projects
- Align solutions with business needs
- Provide analysis and reports to CFM, NCA and VHA on a variety of topics
- Promote optimal construction program management
